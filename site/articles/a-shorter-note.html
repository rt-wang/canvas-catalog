A Shorter Note

November 19, 2025 · 57 words

Not everything needs to be an essay. Short pieces give a catalog its texture —
they sit between the long-form work and stop the whole thing reading like a
seminar.

The build treats a ninety-word note and a four-thousand-word essay identically.
Only the word count in the caption differs, and that is measured for you.
